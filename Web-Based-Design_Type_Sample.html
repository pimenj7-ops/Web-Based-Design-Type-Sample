Exploring Architecture and City Life

Urban design is the process of planning and creating cities, streets, public spaces, parks, and buildings that make everyday life more enjoyable.
It focuses on how people interact with their surroundings and how different elements of a city work together.
Buildings, sidewalks, streetlights, benches, trees, and public transportation all contribute to the character and function of an urban environment.
Good urban design makes places more comfortable, accessible, attractive, and welcoming for everyone.
By observing these details, urban sketchers can learn how cities are organized and how design influences the way people live, work, and connect with one another.
